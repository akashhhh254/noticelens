ℹ️ How NoticeLens Works (100% Explainable & Rule-Based) ▼

1. Rule-Based & 100% Explainable

Zero LLM hallucination, zero external network requests at runtime. Every extracted field maps directly to exact character offsets in the original notice.

2. Date & Year Logic

Numeric dates are parsed as dd/mm. A date missing a year inherits any future year in the notice, or defaults to the next upcoming occurrence.

3. Clashes & Deadlines

Two timed events clash when intervals overlap. Deadlines are points in time: they clash only if they fall strictly inside another event (start ≤ deadline < end). Touching boundaries do not clash.

4. Flag Transparency

When information is missing or inferred (assumed 60 min end, guessed AM/PM, no venue, missing time), amber warning badges show the exact assumption made.
